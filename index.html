The Rescue

Meet Luna.

Luna was found alone, scared, and injured. But someone stopped to help. In that moment, a life began to change.

At Kalinga Animal Welfare, every rescue is treated with dignity, patience, and urgency. We create a path from uncertainty to care, from fear to trust, and from survival to belonging.

The Journey to Recovery

Rescued. Cared for. Ready to thrive.

01

Rescued

Our team responds quickly when an animal is found, injured, abandoned, or in distress.

02

Medical Care

Every rescue receives immediate treatment, nutrition, rest, and skilled care from our support network.

03

Rehabilitation

They regain strength, confidence, and trust through recovery and daily human connection.

04

Ready for Adoption

Once healthy and happy, they are matched with a home where they can begin a new chapter.
